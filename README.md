# Tessera

A home-screen widget maker for Android that a complete beginner can use in two minutes.
Like KWGT, without the formulas, the layer tree or the guesswork about sizing.

**Open source · any Android 8+ phone · no AI · no accounts · no ads · no paid features**

> **Status: planning.** Nothing to install yet. The full product spec, including every decision made so far, is in [`docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md`](docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md).

## What v1 will do

- **Start from a template, then tap anything to change it.** The gallery shows every template live, with your real time, battery and weather.
- **Show what matters to you:** clock, date, battery, weather, your photos (single or slideshow), "days until…" countdowns, a daily quote, a to-do list, a habit streak.
- **No formulas.** Text holds tappable chips like `[Time · 24h]`. Logic is plain "When battery is below 20% → make it red" rules, which you can test with a *Try it* slider.
- **Clocks that are never wrong.** Anything that ticks is drawn by your launcher itself, so it keeps time even when Tessera isn't running.
- **One theme for your whole home screen,** optionally following your wallpaper colors.
- **Share widgets** as a file or a QR code, and **import your old KWGT presets.**
